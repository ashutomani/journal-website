About the Journal

The Journal of Applied and Integrative Biology (JAIB) is a peer-reviewed, biannual, open-access international journal dedicated to publishing high-quality research across the broad spectrum of life sciences. The journal serves as a multidisciplinary platform integrating knowledge from diverse biological disciplines to advance the understanding of complex biological systems.

JAIB encourages the convergence of traditional and emerging areas of biology, including molecular and cellular biology, biotechnology, environmental sciences, computational biology, agriculture, and health-related life sciences. By promoting integrative approaches, the journal seeks to foster innovative research that transcends disciplinary boundaries.

The journal publishes original research articles, review articles, short communications, technical reports, and perspective papers. All submissions undergo a rigorous single-blind peer-review process to ensure scientific quality, originality, and integrity.

As an open-access journal, all articles published in JAIB are freely available to readers worldwide, ensuring maximum visibility and impact of published research. The journal publishes articles exclusively in the English language.

This is an open access journal. All articles published in the Journal of Applied and Integrative Biology (JAIB) are licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0) , which permits unrestricted use, distribution, and reproduction in any medium, provided the original work is properly cited.

The Journal of Applied and Integrative Biology is committed to upholding the highest standards of publication ethics and follows the guidelines of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE). The journal strives to maintain transparency, fairness, and academic integrity throughout the publication process.
