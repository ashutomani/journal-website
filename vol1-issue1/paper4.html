Conclusion  
The current work proves the usefulness of  
the combined CADD methodology in the  
identification of the possible natural GLP-1  
receptor agonists to treat the type 2 diabetes  
mellitus. Virtual screening and molecular  
docking have identified a number of  
compounds that have good binding affinities  
and good pharmacokinetic characteristics.
